## Introduction

At NASA's Quantum Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (QuAIL), we've developed methodologies for fairly benchmarking quantum machine learning algorithms against classical baselines. This post shares our approach.

## Benchmarking Challenges

Comparing quantum and classical ML is non-trivial because:

- Different computational models
- Hardware limitations on quantum side
- Varying problem encodings
- Fair resource allocation

## Our Methodology

### 1. Problem Selection

Choose problems where:

- Quantum advantage is theoretically possible
- Classical solutions exist for comparison
- Practical relevance

### 2. Fair Comparison

Consider:

- Total computation time
- Hardware requirements
- Energy consumption
- Solution quality

### 3. Metrics

- **Accuracy:** Solution quality
- **Time to solution:** Wall clock time
- **Resource usage:** Qubits, gates, classical compute
- **Scalability:** Performance vs. problem size
