## Introduction

At NASA, we're exploring how hybrid quantum-classical architectures can enhance Earth observation applications. This post shares practical insights from our research on integrating quantum processors with classical deep learning systems.

## Why Hybrid?

Current quantum hardware has limitations:

- Limited qubit count (50-100 qubits)
- Short coherence times
- High error rates
- Restricted connectivity

Hybrid approaches leverage:

- Classical systems for high-dimensional data
- Quantum processors for specific computational advantages
- Best of both paradigms

## Architecture Design

Our typical hybrid pipeline:

1. **Classical preprocessing:** Feature extraction from satellite imagery
2. **Quantum processing:** Latent space optimization or sampling
3. **Classical postprocessing:** Final predictions and refinement

## NASA Use Cases

- Wildfire detection with quantum-enhanced segmentation
- Hurricane tracking with quantum sampling
- Climate pattern analysis
